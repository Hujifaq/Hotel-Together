# Hotel Together
A four-player co-op game about running a hotel that turns into a zombie shelter every night.

# Main menu art
Clay parallax art set (hotel background, housekeeper, bellhop, guest and zombie guest), made for Hotel Together with built-in AI image generation.

# Font

# Sound and music
Sound effects and music synthesized for Hotel Together.

# Engine
Made with Unity 6 and the Universal Render Pipeline.
