# Operational hotel elevators

HotelLobby contains two physical cabins in the existing shafts: Guest elevator at x=3 and Service elevator at x=7. Both serve Lobby (0.025), 2 Guest Rooms (4.525), and 3 Rooftop (9.025). Old service-lift teleport panels are removed from the authored scene.

Use E on the hall UP / DOWN button, wait for the doors, walk or push the trolley inside, then use E on a floor button. The cabin has only Lobby, 2 and 3 floor keys. Doors start and reset closed, open on a hall call or arrival, and close after a three-second dwell when boarding/exiting has finished and the threshold sensor is clear. An idle closed cabin never opens on a timer. Pending bot hall calls are retained until boarding. The player can look and walk inside the moving cabin. The cabin carries pushed and parked trolleys, loaded owner-tagged luggage, held supplies and loose rigidbodies already inside at departure. Dropping/releasing items during a trip preserves their transport. Menu pause freezes movement; night alarms do not disable the elevators.

HotelElevator owns queued hall calls and destination requests, door animation/interlocks, continuous eased vertical movement, arrival indicators and spatial motor/chime audio. Landing doors remain closed whenever the cabin is at another floor. CharacterController movement is offset by cabin motion without a fullscreen fade or repositioning to another landing.

HotelGuestElevatorPassenger extends the existing guest bot. Cross-floor routine routes reserve a hall queue position, walk to the lift, board and exit sequentially, then restore the NavMeshAgent at the destination landing and continue the existing activity. Guests prefer the public lift and use the service lift when its estimated queue is shorter. Each cabin supports four bot slots, reduced when the player or a trolley is inside. Hall calls remain pending until waiting guests actually board. If no usable lift has a complete path to its queue, normal stair routing is retained. Survival routes can use lifts at night; danger during an onboard trip retains transport and resumes refuge decisions after exiting.
