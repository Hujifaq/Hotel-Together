# Employee management

Two employees are included in every career. Existing hired staff are retained; the Staff upgrade now supports six additional hires, for eight total employees. Hiring starts at $180 and rises by $100 for each existing hire. Hired employees cost $45 per day each; the two included employees do not add wages. Hiring and duty changes save the existing career ledger without resetting its balance.

Press U, then MANAGE EMPLOYEES. Each row shows current assignment, status and completed job count. Toggle ON DUTY / RESTING, or cycle ALL, Reception, Service, Housekeeping, Maintenance and Delivery. ALL automatically selects available work. Duty changes release reservations and safely drop carried guest cargo. Staff use the selected player employee prefab with a visible head and separate walking, carrying and work poses.

Employees claim one job at a time. Reception requests real cash, waits for the guest's offer, settles its registered receipt, and only then completes check-in. Service fetches the matching meal, water or towel from its station. Housekeeping walks to litter and wet floors. Maintenance handles fire, burst pipes, power failures, lift faults and water outages through the shared staged repair logic; pump restart still requires power. Delivery carries actual tagged luggage to the correct doorstep and knocks for the guest to collect it. Laundry consumes a real detergent dose, uses the real washer timer and returns clean linen after knocking. Helpers also revive fallen guests using shared medicine and repair damaged exterior defenses using shared wood.

Staff follow complete hotel NavMesh paths, including stairs. They do not teleport between floors or complete a job at a distance. Unreachable work is released for retry; blocked room doors remain a constraint. Tool/equipment animations use the existing models and procedural rigs. Staff repairs run slower than the player to retain useful player work.

Native validation reports are in `output/staff-qa`. Purchases, reservation ownership, actor navigation, roles, insufficient funds, repair range, staged pump recovery, autonomous reception cash/check-in and autonomous repair dispatch are covered. Other task families share the existing production work APIs, with staff-specific proximity and inventory checks.

The staff presentation now uses the same bounded two-bone arm and foot solver as the player. Separate poses cover pickup, one-hand suitcase carry, two-hand supplies, mopping, serving, receiving cash, work, knocking, placing and rescue. Suitcases attach by their handle; supplies follow the posed hands; the mop head sweeps along the floor. Cash offered to a worker faces that worker and travels into the receiving hand before the receipt settles and check-in is authorized. Live frame checks cover the visible transfer, check-in ordering, bounded arm reach and clearing an actual wet spot.
